Windows and workspaces

App rules

Tell Slate where an app's windows go and how they open: your music player always on workspace 4, the calculator always floating, a game always full screen. There are none to start with, so every app opens as usual.

Adding a rule

Open  Settings → App Rules and, under Add a rule, open Choose an app. Type to find the app; those with a window open are at the top, marked "open now". The new rule gets a card of its own, named after the app.

What a rule can do

SettingWhat it does
Opens onAny workspace (as usual), or workspace 1 to 10. Slate takes you there when the window opens.
Opens asAs usual, floating (in the middle of the screen), tiled (even on a workspace where windows float), maximized (filling the screen up to the bar) or full screen (over the bar and the dock).
MonitorAny monitor, or one of yours. Shown when you have more than one screen.

A rule takes effect the next time the app opens a window; windows already open stay where they are. Remove this rule, at the bottom of its card, asks first, and the app goes back to opening its windows as it likes. You can add the rule back at any time.

Rules find an app by its window class, the name it gives its windows. Most apps use the same name as their entry in the launcher; the few that don't can be added while they're open, from the top of the list. Window rules of your own in ~/.config/slate/hypr/ still apply alongside Slate's.
