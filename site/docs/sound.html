Your computer

Sound

Where your sound plays, which microphone listens, and Slate's own quiet sounds. The volume is always a click away on the menu bar, and everything else is in  Settings → Sound.

Volume on the menu bar

Click the volume on the menu bar for a panel with the volume, the speakers or headphones it plays through, and your microphone. Scroll over the volume icon to change it without opening anything.

While sound is playing through something, or an app is listening to a microphone, soft stripes drift along its slider, and fade away when it stops. It's an easy way to tell when something's using your mic.

Volume keys

Your keyboard's volume, mute and microphone mute keys work as you'd expect, even while your screen is locked, with a small pop-up showing the new level. Play, pause, next and previous keys control whatever's playing.

Speakers and headphones

Under Output in Settings:

SettingWhat it does
Play sound throughThe speakers or headphones your sound comes out of.
VolumeHow loud they are.
MuteSilence them, without losing the volume you had.

Microphones

Under Input:

SettingWhat it does
MicrophoneWhich microphone apps hear you through.
Input levelHow loud you sound to them.
Mute the microphoneNothing gets through until you unmute it.

The control centre has quick switches for Mute and Mute microphone too (Control centre and panels).

Sound effects

Slate has a small family of soft, rounded sounds, each for one kind of moment. Each has its own volume slider under Sound effects, from off upwards. Press the play button beside one to hear it, and you'll hear it again as you let go of its slider.

SoundWhen you hear it
Volume keysA short tick at the new volume when you press the volume keys.
NotificationsA soft chime when a notification pops up. It stays quiet during Do Not Disturb.
Questions and warningsA soft, questioning sound when Slate asks you to confirm something, an app asks for your password, or a notification is urgent.
ClicksA barely-there tap when you press Slate's buttons, menus and switches. It needs Qt Multimedia (qt6-multimedia) so the click lands right on time; Settings says so if it's missing.
SwitchingA soft swish when you change workspace or move through the window switcher and the overview, drifting the way you're going.
WelcomeA gentle, warm swell when you log in (not when Slate reloads).
GoodbyeA gentle closing sound when you log out, restart or shut down. It plays first, so those take a moment longer.

The lock screen and the login screen are always silent.

When Private Mode is on

While Private Mode is on, every microphone is muted and stays muted, so the Sound page says so at the top, with a Turn off button. Your speakers and Slate's sounds work as usual. When you turn Private Mode off, your microphones go back to just how they were.
