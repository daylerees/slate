Your computer

People on this computer

Everyone who uses this computer can have their own account, with their own files, settings and password. Add, change and remove them from Settings.

Who's here

Open  Settings → Users. Everyone with an account is listed, you first, with their picture, whether they're an administrator, and whether they're logged in right now.

Adding someone or changing their account asks for an administrator's password. You can always change your own name, picture and password.

Adding someone

  1. Under Add someone, click Add.
  2. Type their Full name. Slate suggests a Username from it: what they log in with, and the name of their home folder. It can't be changed later.
  3. Choose a Password and type it twice. Let them know what it is: they can change it later.
  4. Switch on Administrator if they should be able to add and remove people, install apps and change settings for the whole computer.
  5. Click Add, and type your password.

They show up on the login screen straight away.

Changing someone's account

Click Edit beside them:

SettingWhat it does
NameHow they're shown on the login screen and the lock screen. Press Enter to save it.
PictureChoose a picture of theirs. It's cropped to a square for you.
AdministratorWhether they can change the whole computer. There's always at least one administrator, so the last one can't be switched off.
PasswordType the new password twice, then click Change.

Your own picture is the same one as on  Settings → General.

Removing someone

Click Remove beside them, then Remove again to confirm. They won't be able to log in anymore, but their home folder stays where it is, so nothing they made is lost. You can delete it yourself later if you're sure.



Someone who's logged in can't be removed until they log out, and you can't remove your own account while you're using it.

Accounts go through AccountsService (the accountsservice package), the same on every distro. If it isn't running, the page says so.
