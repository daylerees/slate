Apps and tools

Clipboard and emoji

Get back something you copied a while ago, or find just the right emoji. Both open in a small window like the launcher, and both copy what you choose, ready to paste.

Clipboard history

Press Super + V for the last 100 things you copied, text and images, newest first. Type to search them.

  • Enter or a click copies one again, ready to paste wherever you like.
  • Delete forgets the one that's selected.
  • Clear all forgets everything in it.

You can also add a clipboard item to your menu bar, in Edit layout from the bar's right-click menu (Arranging it your way). Its panel shows the latest things you copied, to copy again or clear.



Passwords copied from a password manager are never kept. Your history stays on your computer, in your own home folder.

Switching it off

In  Settings → General → Clipboard, switch off Clipboard history and nothing you copy is kept from then on. Copying and pasting still work as usual. Anything kept before stays until you clear it, with Clear clipboard history just below. There's a Clipboard history quick switch for the control centre too.

Emoji

Press Super + . for the emoji picker. Type a word to search, like "heart" or "tree", use the arrow keys or the pointer to choose, and press Enter or click to copy it. The ones you use most recently come first, so your favourites are always close at hand.



In the launcher, type a colon and a name (:smile) to find an emoji there too.
