Hot corners

Rest the pointer in a corner of your screen and Slate does something for you: opens the overview, locks the screen, and so on. None are set to start with, so nothing happens by surprise.

Setting them up

Open  Settings → Hot Corners. A picture of your screen shows what each corner does. Click a corner on it, then choose its action from the list below:

  • Nothing
  • Lock screen
  • App launcher
  • Workspace overview
  • Control centre
  • Notifications
  • Turn screen off

Each corner can do one thing, and you can give all four something different.

Timing

Delay, under Timing, is how long the pointer rests in a corner before it acts: half a second to start with, anywhere from straight away to a second and a half. A longer delay means fewer surprises when you're just passing through.

A corner acts once each time you visit it. Move the pointer out and back in to use it again.

Good to know

  • Hot corners are the corners of your main display. With more than one display, choose which is the main one in  Settings → Displays → Main display.
  • While the screen's locked, the corners rest. If you unlock with the pointer still in the lock corner, it waits until you've moved away, so you won't lock again by accident.
  • After Turn screen off, the screen comes back on with your next mouse movement or key press, once you've paused for a moment.
