Your computer

Keyboard, mouse and touchpad

How your keyboard types, how your pointer moves, and what your touchpad does, all in  Settings → Input. Anything you haven't changed there follows Hyprland's own settings.

Keyboard layouts

Under Keyboard, Add a layout searches by language or name, like French or Dvorak. Your layouts are listed underneath, and the first one is used first. Remove one you don't need with the button beside it (you always keep at least one).

Switching between them

With more than one layout, Switch layouts with sets the keys that move to the next: Alt + Shift, Ctrl + Space, Caps Lock, or no shortcut at all.

The menu bar shows the layout you're on. Click it for a panel with all your layouts to choose from, or scroll over it to move to the next one. See The menu bar.

Caps Lock and key repeat

SettingWhat it does
Caps Lock keyKeep it as Caps Lock, or make it another Ctrl, Esc, or nothing at all. If it's switching your layouts, it does that instead.
Repeat delayHow long a held key waits before it starts repeating. 500 ms to start with.
Repeat speedHow fast a held key repeats. 16 a second to start with.

Mouse

SettingWhat it does
Pointer speedSlower to faster, for mice and touchpads alike. 0 is the device's own speed.
AccelerationQuick movements carry the pointer further. Switch it off and the pointer moves the same distance however fast you move.
Natural scrollingContent moves the way the wheel turns, like a touch screen.

Pointer style and size

Pointer style chooses the pointer's look: Slate's own, or any pointer theme you have installed. It's used everywhere at once, by Slate, Hyprland and your apps. Pointer size runs from 16 to 64 px. Apps you open from then on use it too, so one that was already open may keep the old size until you open it again.

Under More pointer styles are a few good themes you can add in one click, with no password needed. There's more about them in Icons and pointers.



Over anything in Slate you can click, the pointer turns into a hand, so it's easy to tell what's a button.

Touchpad

On a computer with a touchpad, a Touchpad section appears:

SettingWhat it does
Scroll speedHow far a two-finger scroll goes.
Natural scrollingContent follows your fingers, like a phone. On to start with.
Tap to clickA light tap counts as a click.
Ignore while typingStops a palm on the touchpad moving the pointer as you type. Game mode lets the touchpad work while you type, and puts this back when you turn it off (Game mode).

Gestures

Slate sets up a few touchpad gestures for you:

  • Four fingers left or right moves between workspaces.
  • Three fingers up makes the window full screen.
  • Three fingers down closes the window.
  • Three fingers left floats the window, or tiles it again.

Your own input settings

Anything Settings doesn't cover, like settings for one particular device, can go in your own Hyprland config in ~/.config/slate/hypr/, which Slate never overwrites. See Your own config.
