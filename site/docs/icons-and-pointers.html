Look and feel

Icons and pointers

Pick the icons your apps and Slate use, give your folders a colour, and choose a pointer you like. A few well-made icon and pointer styles go in with one click.

Icon theme

In  Settings → Icons → Icon theme, Icons lists every icon theme on your computer that has app icons. The one your computer already had comes first, listed by its name with "(default)" after it, for example "Papirus (default)", so you can always go back to it.

Choosing a theme changes the icons in your apps, GTK, Qt and KDE alike. Apps that are already open pick them up as they can. Slate's own dock, launcher and bar redraw themselves in the new icons a moment later.

If the theme you chose is uninstalled later, your apps go back to your computer's own and Slate says so on the row.

Folder colour

Folder colour colours the folders in Nautilus, Dolphin and other file managers:

  • From the wallpaper: the colour closest to your wallpaper's, changing as it changes.
  • Theme's own, the default: the folders as your icon theme draws them.
  • A colour of your choice, from the ones your icons come in.

It works with icon themes whose folders come in colours: Papirus, Kora and Nordzy. Desktop, Home, Documents and the other special folders take the same colour. With another theme, the row says it needs one of these.



Slate never changes your system's icons. It makes a small icon theme of its own in your home folder that only swaps the folders, so going back to Theme's own is always clean.

App icons

 Settings → Icons → App icons sets how app icons look in the dock, launcher, switcher, bar and notifications:

SettingWhat it does
ShapeTile (the default), Squircle, Circle, Square or Original, the icon's own shape.
Fill the shapeCrops each icon's own border and shadow and grows it to fill the shape, on its main colour. Off, the whole icon sits smaller on a plain tile. With Original, it trims empty margins and theme shadows so every icon is the same size.
Tiles in the icon's colourThe tile behind each icon takes a deep shade of the icon's own main colour rather than the theme's. Grey icons keep the theme's tile. For icons on a tile only.
Tint app iconsColours app icons with your accent. Tint strength goes from a light wash of colour to icons drawn entirely in the accent.

With Rounded UI elements off, app icons keep the shape you chose here.

More icon styles

Under More icon styles, a few well-made icon themes are ready to add with one click:

StyleWhat it's like

MoreWaitaGNOME's own look, with icons for many more apps
Numix CircleBright, round app icons, in light or dark
ZafiroFlat, soft and pastel, in dark or light

Click Install and Slate downloads it from its makers' own release, checks it's exactly the version Slate chose, and puts it in your own icons folder (~/.local/share/icons). There's no password to type, and nothing from it is run. A notification says when it's ready; then choose it in Icon theme whenever you like.



If an install doesn't work out, Slate tells you. Check you're connected to the internet and try again.

Pointers

Your pointer's settings live with the mouse, in  Settings → Input → Mouse:

  • Pointer style: Slate (default), Slate's own pointer, or any pointer theme on your computer. Hyprland and your apps all follow it; apps opened from now on use it.
  • Pointer size: from 16 to 64 px.

Over buttons, switches and links in Slate's panels and windows, the pointer becomes a hand. Over the desktop, the dock and right-click menus, where everything is there to be clicked, it stays an arrow.
