Windows and workspaces

Spaces

Set up your apps across workspaces the way you like them, then save them as a space. Next time, one click brings them all back, each window in its place.

Saving a space

Click Spaces on the menu bar, next to Apps, then New Space.

  1. Pick the workspaces to save. Each workspace with apps on it shows as a small live preview; click the ones you want, and a tick marks them.
  2. Give it a name, like Coding, Writing or Gaming.
  3. Choose an icon and a colour: one of the pastels, or any colour of your own from the colour wheel or a hex code.

A space keeps the apps on those workspaces, where their windows sit (tiled, or floating with their place and size), each workspace's window mode and its name.

Saving under a name you already use replaces that space, and Slate tells you before you do. If there's nothing open yet, the panel says so: open the apps you'd like, arrange them, then come back.

Bringing one back

Click a space in the Spaces panel. Each of its workspaces opens as a new one, its apps start, and their windows go back to the same tiles, floating spots and sizes. Your other workspaces and windows stay just as they are.

To remove a space, point at its row and click the delete button that takes the place of its apps. Slate asks first.



To bring a space back from a key binding or a script, use slate ipc call spaces restore <name>. See The command line.

How its windows are marked

Windows opened from a space wear its colours, so you can tell at a glance where they belong:

  • Top border: a thin line in the space's colour along the top of each window.
  • Corner badge: the space's icon in the window's top left corner. It moves across to the other corner when the pointer comes near, so it's never in your way.
  • On the menu bar: the window's icon sits on the space's colour.
  • In the switchers: the space's icon on its windows in the window switcher and the workspace overview.

Each of these can be switched off in  Settings → Spaces: Colour the window's icon under Menu Bar, and Top border, Corner badge and Badges in the switchers under Windows from a space.
