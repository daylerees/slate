Windows and workspaces

Switching windows

Hold Super and press Tab to see every open window in a strip across the middle of the screen. Let go of Super and you're there.

Using it

  • Open it: hold Super and press Tab. The window you were in a moment ago is already chosen, so a quick Super + Tab flips between your last two windows.
  • Move along: keep holding Super and press Tab again, or the right arrow.
  • Go back: add Shift (Super + Shift + Tab), or use the left arrow.
  • Switch: let go of Super, press Enter, or click a window.
  • Change your mind: press Esc, or click outside the strip, and you stay where you were.

For a plainer way round, Alt + Tab goes straight to the next window without the switcher.

What it shows

Every window on your workspaces, most recently used first, in a strip that loops round so you can go either way. Each card has the window's title under it. Below the strip, your workspace dots show which workspace the chosen window is on, with the marker sliding as you move.

Windows on the secret workspace stay out of the switcher. A window that came from a space wears its space's icon on its card (you can turn that off in Settings → Spaces).

Settings

In  Settings → Windows → Window switcher (Super+Tab):

SettingChoices
Windows showLive previews of each window's contents; App icons, each window's outline with its app's icon (nothing is captured, which is lighter); or Both, a live preview with a small app icon on the corner.
Moving between windowsSlide (the strip slides along, the chosen window in the middle), Hop, Wheel (round the edge of a turning wheel), Tilt (the others turned away in 3D), Orbit (round a 3D ring), Deck (dealt from a pile), Fan (spread like a hand of cards) or Instant.
Going to the windowCrossfade (a window on another workspace fades in over the one you're on), Workspace animation (your usual workspace switch), or Instant.


In game mode the switcher shows app icons rather than live previews, to keep your game running smoothly.

Moving between workspaces instead

To see whole workspaces rather than single windows, use the workspace overview, Super + ↓. See Workspaces and the overview.
