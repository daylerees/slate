Look and feel

Wallpaper

Your wallpaper is on your desktop, your lock screen and the login screen. If you like, it can shape Slate's colours too, so everything feels of a piece.

Choosing one

Open  Settings → Wallpaper, or right-click the desktop and choose Change wallpaper…. Your pictures show as thumbnails; click one to use it, and the old wallpaper crossfades into the new. Random picks one for you.

For a quick change without opening Settings, right-click the desktop and choose Next wallpaper.

The wallpaper switcher

Press Super + Alt + W to see your wallpapers in a row across the lower half of the screen, starting at the one you have. Left and Right (or the scroll wheel, or a click on another) show each one full screen, over your windows, and Slate's colours follow it when they come from the wallpaper. Your apps and windows stay open behind it, and the menu bar and dock stay in sight, resting until it closes.

Enter, or a click on the one chosen, keeps it, just as choosing it in Settings does; that's when your other apps' colours and the login screen catch up. Esc, or a click away from the row, puts your wallpaper and colours back exactly as they were. Pressing the shortcut again while it's open moves to the next one.

Your folder

Slate looks for pictures (JPEG, PNG and WebP) in your Pictures folder to start with. The Folder row shows where it's looking:

  • Open shows the folder in your file manager, so you can add pictures to it.
  • Change… opens a small folder browser right there in Settings. Find the folder you'd like, then click Use this folder.

New pictures you put in the folder show up straight away.

Hiding small images

A folder like Pictures often holds icons, avatars and small screenshots that would make poor wallpapers. Hide small images, on to start with, leaves out anything smaller than Smallest size (1280 × 720 to start with, or anything from 320 × 240 to 2560 × 1440). The count above your thumbnails says how many were hidden.

Slate's own wallpapers

Slate comes with ten wallpapers of its own, in 4K: soft glows of colour over a deep base, each with Slate's mark in the middle or plain. Ember, Iris, Lagoon and Blossom are in mixed colours, then Fern (green), Cobalt (blue), Ruby (red), Honey (yellow), Graphite (black and grey) and Mist (white and grey).

They're listed under From Slate, below your own pictures. Switch off Slate's wallpapers to hide them, and to leave them out of Random and of changing by itself.

Changing by itself

Change wallpaper can switch to another picture for you: At each login, every 15 or 30 minutes, every hour, every 3 hours or every day. It goes through your folder (and Slate's wallpapers, if they're shown) in a shuffled order. Never, the default, keeps the one you pick.

Colours from your wallpaper

Two settings in Appearance let your wallpaper shape Slate's look: Accent colour from wallpaper and Background tint from wallpaper. With either on, the bar, panels, Settings and your themed apps shift together each time the wallpaper changes. Folder colour on the Icons page can follow it too.
