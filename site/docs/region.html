Region and time

The time, written the way you're used to, and the weather for where you are. Most of it is in  Settings → Region.

The clock

At the top of the Region page are today's date and the time, then:

SettingWhat it does
Time zoneWhere you are, for the clock. Type to search, like "London" or "New York".
Set the time automaticallyKeeps your clock right, using a time server on the internet.

Changing either may ask for your password, as they're shared by everyone who uses this computer.

12 or 24-hour, and seconds

How the menu bar's clock looks is part of the bar. Right-click an empty part of the bar and choose Clock for 12 or 24-hour time and seconds, or click the clock in Edit layout to choose whether it shows the date and seconds. See The menu bar.

Formats

SettingChoices
Date formatDay first (Thu 12 Nov, to start with), month first (Thu, Nov 12) or year first (2026-11-12). It's used on the menu bar's clock, the calendar, notifications and everywhere else Slate shows a date.
Week starts onMonday (to start with), Sunday or Saturday, for the clock's calendar.
TemperatureCelsius (to start with) or Fahrenheit, for the weather in the clock's panel and the control centre.

The weather and your location

Slate shows the weather under the calendar in the clock's panel and in the control centre: today as it is now, and the next three days. It needs to know where you are, so it stays hidden until you set your location.

Set it in  Settings → General → Location: start typing your town or city, and pick it from the places that come up. Night light's sunset to sunrise schedule uses it too (Night light).
