Your day

Timers

Two gentle timers: a reminder to drink some water, and a focus timer that keeps you to short, steady rounds with breaks between. Switch them on in  Settings → Timers.

Each can sit on the menu bar, the dock or both (Show on). On the menu bar it's a small icon that opens its panel; on the dock it's a tile with a ring that empties as the time runs down.

Drink reminder

Every so often (45 minutes to start with, Every changes it) a notification says it's time for a drink, and the droplet on the menu bar lights up. Press I've had a drink on the notification, in its panel, or click the dock tile, and the next wait starts. The panel counts how many you've had today.

Focus timer

A Pomodoro: focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break, which starts by itself. After every 4 rounds there's a longer, 15-minute break. All four can be changed in Settings. When a break ends, a notification asks whether you're ready for the next round, and the timer waits for you.

  • Start, Pause and Resume: in its panel, or click the dock tile.
  • Break (while focusing) or Skip (on a break) moves on to the next part.
  • Reset goes back to the start of the first round.
  • Right-click the dock tile for all of these.

With Do Not Disturb while focusing on, notifications wait quietly in the list while you focus, and pop up again on your break.

Both keep time while the computer sleeps, so they're right when you come back.
