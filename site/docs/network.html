Your computer

Network and Bluetooth

Getting online and connecting your headphones, keyboards and other devices. The quickest way is the menu bar, and the full details are in Settings → Network and Settings → Bluetooth.

Wi-Fi

Click the Wi-Fi icon on the menu bar for the networks around you, with a switch to turn Wi-Fi on or off. Click a network to join it.

In  Settings → Network, the Networks list shows each one's signal, and whether it's secured, saved or the one you're on.

Joining a network

  1. Click Connect beside the network.
  2. If it needs a password, a box opens under it: type the password and press Enter (Esc closes it again).

Open networks and ones you've joined before connect straight away. Disconnect leaves a network, and Forget removes a saved one, so it won't join by itself any more.

Your connection

Under Connection, Settings shows how you're connected and the details, for when you need them: your IP address, gateway, DNS, link speed, the Wi-Fi band and channel, its security, and a VPN if you're using one.

Wired connections

Plug in a cable and Slate connects by itself, with nothing to set up. The Wired section of the Network page shows each socket and whether it's connected. While you're on a cable, the menu bar shows a wired icon in place of Wi-Fi, and its panel shows the cable's details.

Airplane Mode

Airplane Mode turns Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and mobile broadband off together. Switch it on from the control centre's quick switches, or type "airplane" in the launcher.

Slate remembers what was on, so when you turn Airplane Mode off, only those come back: if Bluetooth was already off, it stays off. As on a phone, you can still turn Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on by hand while it's on, say for the plane's Wi-Fi, and Airplane Mode stays on until you turn it off.

Bluetooth

Click the Bluetooth icon on the menu bar for your devices, with a switch for Bluetooth itself. Click a device to connect it.

Pairing a new device

  1. Open  Settings → Bluetooth.
  2. Put your device in pairing mode (its instructions will say how; often it's holding a button until a light flashes).
  3. Next to New devices, click Search. Devices nearby appear under Devices.
  4. Click Pair beside yours.

Once it's paired, Connect and Disconnect are beside it, and Forget removes it. You can pair it again later whenever you like.



If Settings says No Bluetooth adapter found, your computer has no Bluetooth. The Bluetooth icon and quick switch stay hidden until there's one to use.
