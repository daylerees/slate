Your computer

Printers and the webcam

Most printers from the last ten years work straight away, over your network or by USB, with no driver to install. And your webcam's picture can be set just the way you like it, for every app at once.

Printers

Open  Settings → Printers. Each printer you've added is listed with what it's doing and any jobs waiting. Beside each one:

  • Make default: the one your apps print to unless you choose another.
  • Test page: prints a page, to check everything's working.
  • Cancel jobs: clears what's waiting to print.
  • Remove: takes the printer off this computer. You can add it again at any time.

Adding a printer

  1. Switch the printer on, and connect it to your network or plug it in.
  2. Under Add a printer, click Search. Slate looks on your network and USB.
  3. Click Add beside yours.

For a printer that needs a driver of its own, or more settings than Slate has, Other printers opens your printer settings app, or the printing service's own page in your browser.

If printing isn't set up

Printing goes through CUPS. If it isn't installed, Settings says so: install the cups package and come back. If it's installed but not running, click Turn on (it asks for your password), or run the command Settings shows in a terminal.

Scanning

Under Scanners, Slate lists the scanners it can find. Scan opens your scanning app, and Look again searches once more if yours was switched off.

Scanning needs SANE (sane-utils or sane-backends, with sane-airscan for network scanners) and a scanning app such as Document Scanner (simple-scan). Settings tells you if either is missing.

The webcam

 Settings → Webcam sets your camera's picture. Changes apply straight away, to every app that uses the camera. Webcams usually forget their settings when they're unplugged or the computer restarts, so Slate keeps yours and puts them back each time you log in.

Preview and who's using it

SettingWhat it does
PreviewShows the camera on the page. Its light comes on only while the preview's showing.
CameraWith more than one, which camera you're setting up.
In use byWhich apps are using the camera right now, if any.

Picture

Brightness, contrast, colour, sharpness, gain (brightens a dim picture, at the cost of more grain) and gamma (brightens or darkens the middle tones). Only the ones your camera supports are shown.

Light

SettingWhat it does
Automatic white balanceAdjusts the colours to the light in the room. Switch it off to set the colour temperature yourself: lower is warmer, higher is cooler.
Automatic exposureAdjusts how bright the picture is to the light. Switch it off to set the exposure yourself: longer is brighter, but blurs movement.
Anti-flickerStops lights flickering in the picture by matching your mains electricity: 50 Hz in the UK, Europe and most of the world, 60 Hz in North America.
Backlight compensationBrightens you when there's a bright light behind you, like a window.

If Settings says No webcam found, check the camera isn't switched off: some laptops have a key or a little slider for it. Then click Look again.

While Private Mode is on, the camera is off for every app, so the Webcam page says so, with a Turn off button, in place of the picture settings. To see when an app turns your camera on, see Knowing when your camera's on.
