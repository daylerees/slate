Your computer

Storage

How full your drives are and what's using the space, with gentle ways to free some:  Settings → Storage.

Drives

Each drive, how much is free, and a bar for how full it is. It turns red once a drive has less than a tenth of its space (or 5 GB) left.

What's using your space

Apps (installed apps and the system), Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Videos, Music, caches, the Waste Basket, and everything else in your home folder. Measuring takes a little while, so the page shows the last measurement straight away and a fresh one when it's done. Below that, Your biggest folders lists the largest folders in your home, each with Open. Hidden ones (their names start with a dot) are kept by apps.

Freeing space

ActionWhat it does
Empty the Waste BasketDeletes what's in it for good, after asking.
Clear old cachesDeletes thumbnails, and apps' temporary files nothing has used for a month, after asking. Your own files stay as they are, and apps make what they need again.

Nearly full

With Warn when a drive is nearly full on (as it is to start with), Slate checks every ten minutes and sends one calm notification when a drive gets down to a tenth of its space or 5 GB. It won't say it again until the drive has had room again.
