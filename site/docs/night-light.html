Night light, game mode and caffeine

Three switches for three moments: warmer colours in the evening, everything fancy out of the way while you play, and a computer that stays awake while you need it.

Night light

Night light warms your screen's colours, which is kinder to your eyes at night. Switch it on with its quick switch in the control centre, or in  Settings → General → Night light.

SettingWhat it does
WarmthThe colour temperature, from 2500 K to 6000 K: lower is warmer. 4000 K to start with.
ScheduleManual: only when you switch it. Sunset to sunrise: on and off with the sun where you are (set your location first, below). Custom times: on and off at times you choose, 20:00 to 07:00 to start with.

With a schedule, switching night light by hand lasts until the next change, then the schedule carries on.

Game mode

Game mode turns off what takes the most work to draw, so your games run as smoothly as they can. Switch it on with its quick switch in the control centre, from the launcher, or with the game mode button you can add to the menu bar (Edit layout).

While it's on, these are off:

  • Glass and blur
  • See-through windows
  • Shadows
  • Animations
  • Live window previews (the switcher and overview show each window's shape and app icon instead)
  • Notification pop-ups (they still go to the list, so you won't miss anything)
  • Nova's answer sound
  • Ignoring the touchpad while you type, since games often use both at once

None of your settings are changed. When you turn game mode off, everything comes back just as you had it.

Caffeine

The Caffeine quick switch in the control centre keeps your computer awake: while it's on, Slate won't start the screensaver, lock the screen, turn it off or suspend for being idle. It's handy for a presentation, a long download or a recipe on screen. Caffeine turns itself off when Slate restarts, so it's never left on by accident.

Caffeine always holds off locking, the screen turning off, sleep and the screensaver, whatever Wait while media plays is set to: that setting is about apps keeping the screen awake, and Caffeine is you asking. The times themselves are in Locking when you're away.

Your picture and location

The rest of  Settings → General is about you:

  • Your picture appears on the control centre, the lock screen and the login screen. Click Change picture to choose one from your picture folders, with a round preview. You can also click your picture in the control centre.
  • Location is used for the weather and for night light's sunset and sunrise. Start typing your town or city and pick it from the list. Until it's set, the weather stays hidden.
  • Clipboard history, and clearing it, are here too. See Clipboard and emoji.
